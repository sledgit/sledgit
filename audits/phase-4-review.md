## What held up

- Event origin is sound. Only receipts the vault itself executed, with non-Failure status and at or before the cutoff, are counted (`parseTxs`). Events logged by other contracts, failed receipts and post-cutoff logs are dropped. The contract only emits `deposit` for owner transfers of policy tokens, and `withdraw` only after the transfer succeeded.
- Determinism of the pure part: compute(inputs) reproduces the committed `derived_sha256` and `inputs_sha256` exactly. Order-independence is tested. A recompute under the Estonian locale is still equal on today's data.
- Replayed holdings equal the on-chain wallet + Rhea + Intents balances for all 8 vaults today.
- Cutoff handling: rules receipts are limited to decisions on chain by the cutoff. Hourly marks carry close time, and unfinished bars are filtered out. A missing minute bar fails the computation. The default cutoff sits 2 minutes back, so minute bars are final. The build fails closed and writes atomically.
- Benchmarks start at each entry's own start time, value and price, so timing the funding does not move the baseline. Flows are neutralised correctly in the middle of a season (P4-1 only covers an empty vault at the end). No exploitable lever was found in the 1-minute reference window: the trade cost and the flow splits use one price on both sides, so the choice cancels out.
- `verifySeason` itself is strong: contiguous seqs, a receipt for every on-chain decision, a per-event binding and evidence-reuse checks. The gap (P4-6) is the league wrapper.
- Ranking gate: legacy-factory children are excluded, and non-genuine vaults, fixtures and entries under 7 days are never ranked. Provenance pins the code hash and requires a keyless vault and a keyless factory.
- Rendering: all agent-supplied strings (notes, names, codes, reasons) are rendered as React text. `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` is used only for static JSON-LD, and links are built from tx hashes and accounts. No injection path was found.
- The testnet caveat is on the leaderboard note, the table cell ("testnet · not performance"), the agent page headline and its vs-Hold line. The exceptions are the copy in P4-18 and the "±" shown on agent pages.
